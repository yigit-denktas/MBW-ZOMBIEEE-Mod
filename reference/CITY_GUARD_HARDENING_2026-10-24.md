## Defect class / intervention

Original City guard-post selector used vertex-level reachability and a simple score near desired bearings, permitting face centroids immediately beside scene props. The original selected posts were centered at (165.500,169.267), (183.272,191.292), (153.814,188.910), with nearest mapped object-anchor gaps of **2.94**, **1.29**, and **1.96** scene units, respectively. A short XY gap from an object's transform does **not** demonstrate an actual collision, but is a measurable obstruction risk.

**Changed `tools/scene_tactics.py`**:
1. Require a chain of **actual scene AI-mesh shared edges** from the survivor-spawn-side face (do not equate arbitrary vertex proximity with walkable faces).
2. Require 2D face area **≥3.0** scene-unit² and scene-prop *origin* clearance **≥3.0** scene units. Both are conservative geometric proxies, not true collision bounds or sight lines.
3. Preserve survivor-spawn distance **9–24**, original enemy-spawn distance **≥28**, max elevation delta **2.5**, minimum post-to-post distance **>10**, and deterministic bearing/coordinate tie breaking.
4. Fail closed with `SceneError` if eligible positions disappear, rather than choosing a cluttered/disconnected fallback or silently changing other maps.
5. Report `nav_reachable_faces`, selected `nearest_prop_anchor`, `min_face_xy_area`, `min_prop_anchor_clearance`, and the existing explicit no-gameplay-verification limitation. Original scene assets are never edited in place.

The original City mesh decodes to **323 vertices, 600 edges, 270 faces**; **201** AI faces are in the edge-connected component of survivor spawn. All three posts remain on that component and maintain distinct reserved entry IDs 2/3/4. Zombie entries 32–40 and the complete original 25 native triggers are untouched.
